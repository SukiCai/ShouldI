OVERVIEW: CANADIAN PM COMPENSATION STRUCTURE

Canadian PM compensation differs from US compensation in two important ways:
1. Base salary is the primary comp component; equity (RSUs) is significant only at Shopify, US-parented companies, and well-funded scale-ups
2. Total comp is 25–40% lower in USD terms than comparable US roles

Compensation components in Canada:
- Base salary (primary): 80–90% of total cash comp for most Canadian roles
- Annual bonus: 5–15% of base for corporate/finance tech; 0% to 10% for pure tech startups
- RSUs (Restricted Stock Units): Common at Shopify, Amazon Canada, Google Canada, Microsoft Canada; less common at Canadian-founded startups without recent funding rounds
- Benefits: Standard dental/medical (supplements provincial healthcare — does not replace it); RRSP employer matching 3–5% common at larger employers

TORONTO (Ontario):

Associate PM / Junior PM (0–3 years):
- Median base: CAD $80,000–$95,000
- Total comp (with bonus): CAD $82,000–$100,000
- Robert Half range: $74,000–$104,000

PM / Product Manager (3–6 years):
- Median base: CAD $105,000–$125,000
- Total comp (with bonus): CAD $108,000–$135,000
- Robert Half range: $98,000–$138,000

Senior PM (6–10 years, IC track):
- Median base: CAD $130,000–$155,000
- Total comp: CAD $135,000–$175,000 (RSUs add significantly at Shopify/Amazon Canada)
- Robert Half range: $120,000–$165,000

Principal / Staff PM:
- Median base: CAD $160,000–$195,000
- Total comp: CAD $165,000–$225,000+ (dependent on RSU grants)

Director of Product:
- Median base: CAD $175,000–$220,000
- Total comp: CAD $185,000–$260,000+

VP of Product:
- Range: CAD $220,000–$300,000+ (mostly at public companies or well-funded scale-ups)

VANCOUVER (British Columbia):

Salary levels are comparable to Toronto within ±5–10%, but housing costs are higher. The Vancouver tech scene includes Hootsuite, Clio, Slack Canada, Electronic Arts, and Amazon's large Vancouver office.

PM (3–6 years):
- Median base: CAD $100,000–$120,000
- Amazon Vancouver PM: CAD $120,000–$145,000 base + RSUs

Senior PM (6–10 years):
- Median base: CAD $125,000–$155,000
- Amazon/Google/Microsoft Vancouver: CAD $140,000–$175,000 base + RSUs

Director of Product, Vancouver:
- Median base: CAD $175,000–$215,000

NATIONAL MEDIAN (Government of Canada Job Bank):
NOC 20012 — Computer and Information Systems Managers:
- National median: CAD $112,000

COMPANY-SPECIFIC DATA (APPROXIMATE, FROM GLASSDOOR CA AND COMMUNITY SOURCES)

- PM: CAD $120,000–$145,000 base + significant RSU grant
- Senior PM: CAD $145,000–$175,000 + RSU

Note: Shopify RSU vesting schedule: 4 years, 25%/year. At current Shopify market cap, RSU grants are meaningful compensation component.

Amazon Canada (Toronto / Vancouver):
- PMT / APM: CAD $100,000–$120,000 base + RSU (lower base, higher equity)
- PM I: CAD $120,000–$140,000 + RSU
- PM II: CAD $140,000–$165,000 + RSU

Note: Amazon's compensation is front-loaded with RSUs that vest over 4 years (uneven vesting schedule). Year 1 is lowest; Years 3–4 are highest. Total comp including RSUs at Senior PM can reach CAD $200,000–$230,000.

Google Canada (Toronto / Waterloo):
- PM II: CAD $135,000–$160,000 base + bonus + RSU
- Senior PM: CAD $160,000–$195,000 + bonus + RSU

Microsoft Canada:
- PM I/II: CAD $110,000–$140,000

Major Canadian Banks (Digital/Fintech divisions):

US vs. CANADA COMPARISON (SAME CAREER STAGE)

For a Senior PM with 6–9 years experience:

San Francisco / Seattle (US):
- Total comp: USD $200,000–$380,000 (FAANG)
- Non-FAANG: USD $140,000–$220,000

Toronto / Vancouver (Canada):
- Total comp: CAD $155,000–$230,000 = USD $113,000–$168,000 (at 0.73 CAD/USD)

Gap: US FAANG total comp is approximately 2–3× Canadian total comp in USD terms for comparable seniority.
Gap: Non-FAANG US vs. Canadian is approximately 1.2–1.5× in USD terms.

Why the gap exists and why it persists:
1. Smaller market: Canadian companies serve Canadian/North American market — revenue base is smaller → compensation budgets are smaller
2. Equity at Canadian companies: Non-public Canadian companies' equity is illiquid with uncertain upside; US public company RSUs are liquid from vest date
3. Tax: Canadian marginal rates are higher (43–49% marginal in Ontario at $150K+) vs. US federal only (37% marginal at >$578K; most tech workers in US are 22–24% marginal)
4. Historical convention: Canadian comp compression has structural inertia; US companies bidding for Canadian talent have slowly raised the floor but not eliminated the gap

THE "SHOULD I ACCEPT A CANADIAN ROLE" CALIBRATION

For a US-based PM considering a Canadian opportunity:
- If the Canadian role is significantly more senior than what you'd get in the US (scope and title jump): may be worth the comp reduction
- If it's a lateral move with comp cut: Only makes sense if immigration security (Canadian PR) is the primary goal
- US→Canada comp cut is real and should be explicitly modeled: a $200K USD offer vs. a CAD $150K offer is approximately a 46% total comp reduction

For a Canadian-based PM evaluating whether to stay:
- The gap is real but not unbridgeable via level progression
- A Principal PM or Director in Canada at a major employer can achieve CAD $220,000–$280,000, closing the gap relative to mid-level US roles
- The question is whether the immigration security, quality of life, and career scope in Canada justify the gap vs. US or home country alternatives
