## Key Observations for PM Career Advice

1. **National median ~$125K CAD** is materially below equivalent US roles (USD $180K–$250K base for Senior PM at mid-tier US tech), but total comp gap narrows when accounting for Canadian healthcare, lower student debt norms, and PR timeline security.

2. **Ontario (Toronto) has the highest "high" wage ceiling at $132.57/hr (~$276K/yr)** — driven by FAANG Canada offices (Amazon, Google, Microsoft) and large Canadian fintech/banking tech employers (RBC, TD, Shopify).

3. **BC ceiling is lower ($89.25/hr high vs. $132.57 in Ontario)** despite Vancouver being a major tech hub — reflects fewer Principal/Staff PM seats and lower concentration of top-paying employers relative to Toronto.

4. **Alberta median ($65.00/hr) exceeds Ontario median ($60.10/hr)** despite smaller tech sector — likely driven by energy-sector PM roles and smaller sample size.

5. **The "high" wage band in Toronto ($147.06/hr = ~$306K/yr)** represents top-of-market senior/principal PMs at FAANG Canada and major financial institutions — this is the ceiling for Canadian PM comp without relocating to US.

---

## Data Notes

- NOC 60010 (Corporate sales managers) is the official Canadian classification for Product Managers; it is broader than the tech-specific usage
- Data from Labour Force Survey (national/Ontario) and Small Area Estimation model (provinces/regions)
- Annual salary estimates use 2,080 hours/year (40 hrs/week × 52 weeks); actual salaries may differ due to overtime, bonuses, and equity compensation not captured in LFS wage data
- Equity (RSUs, options) at FAANG Canada offices and tech unicorns (Shopify) can add CAD $20K–$100K+ to total comp above these wage figures
