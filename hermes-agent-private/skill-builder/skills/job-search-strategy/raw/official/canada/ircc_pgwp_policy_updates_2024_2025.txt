## PGWP Duration Policy — Updated February 15, 2024

IRCC implemented major PGWP changes effective February 15, 2024:

### New Duration Rules (post-Feb 15, 2024)
- **Master's degree (any length)**: 3-year PGWP regardless of program duration
  - Previously: a 1-year master's → 1-year PGWP. Now: any master's → 3-year PGWP
- **Doctoral (PhD) degree (any length)**: 3-year PGWP regardless of program duration
- **Other programs 8 months to under 2 years**: PGWP = length of program
- **Programs 2 years or longer**: PGWP = 3 years (unchanged)

**Impact**: International students who completed 1-year master's programs now receive the same 3-year PGWP as graduates of 2-year programs. This is a significant advantage — 3 years of open work authorization gives ample time to accumulate 1 year of skilled Canadian work experience needed for Canadian Experience Class (CEC).

### Field of Study Requirements (Bachelor's Degree Level — post-Nov 1, 2024)
As of November 1, 2024, bachelor's degree graduates must have studied in an eligible field to receive a PGWP. (Master's and PhD graduates are exempt from the field of study requirement and receive the 3-year PGWP automatically.)

**Fields added (newly eligible for bachelor's graduates)**:
- Agriculture and agri-food
- Healthcare: nursing, pharmacy, medical laboratory technology, dental hygiene, paramedicine
- Social services and community work
- Early childhood education and care
- Education (teacher training)
- Skilled trades: electricians, plumbers, HVAC technicians
- Transportation: aviation maintenance, marine engineering

**Fields removed (no longer PGWP-eligible for bachelor's level, effective Nov 1, 2024)**:
- 178 fields removed including many general business, liberal arts, and some social science programs
- Students in affected programs who started before Nov 1, 2024 may have transitional protection

**Key rule**: If you already hold a master's or PhD — field of study doesn't matter. You get a 3-year PGWP.

## PGWP Processing Time (2024-2025)

- IRCC reports current PGWP processing time: approximately **8 months** (as of mid-2025)
- Students should apply for PGWP as early as possible after graduation
- While PGWP application is pending, graduates can work under the "maintained status" provision if they applied before their study permit expired

**Tactic**: Apply for PGWP the same week as graduation. The 8-month wait is real — students who wait months before applying are burning their PGWP clock before it's even issued.

## CRITICAL POLICY CHANGE: LMIA Job Offer Points Removed from Express Entry CRS

**Effective: Spring 2025 (announced December 18, 2024 by Minister Marc Miller)**

### What changed
Express Entry candidates can no longer receive additional CRS points for holding an LMIA-backed job offer. IRCC removed the arranged employment points from the Comprehensive Ranking System.

### Previous point structure (no longer in effect)
- 50 CRS points for a valid LMIA-backed job offer (any NOC level)
- 200 CRS points for a valid LMIA-backed job offer in executive positions (NOC TEER 0, major group 00)

### Why IRCC made this change
Minister Miller stated the measure aims to "remove the incentive for candidates to purchase an LMIA, resulting in increased fairness and integrity in Express Entry." The government found that some immigration consultants were selling fraudulent or inflated LMIA letters to boost CRS scores — the 200-point executive LMIA bonus in particular had become a vector for fraud.

### What still gives CRS points for job offers
IRCC announced plans to reintroduce job offer points for "high-wage occupations" with Canadian work experience — but this replacement mechanism had not been implemented as of spring 2025. The current state: no job offer points available through LMIA route.

### Impact on international students
For PGWP holders pursuing Express Entry/CEC, this change:
1. **Removes a shortcut**: Previously, securing an LMIA-backed offer could jump a candidate's CRS score by 50-200 points. That path is closed.
2. **Levels the playing field**: Candidates compete purely on core CRS factors: language (CLB/IELTS), education, age, Canadian work experience, and provincial nomination.
3. **Increases importance of French**: French language bonus (still in effect) is now one of the most impactful CRS optimizations remaining. TEF Canada or TCF Canada scores can add 25-50+ points.
4. **Increases PNP importance**: Provincial Nominee Programs that add 600 points (guaranteeing ITA) become more valuable when you can't close a CRS gap via job offer.

## PGWP Growth Statistics (IRCC Data)

- **2023**: 243,294 new PGWP holders — up 214% from 74,480 in 2018
- **75%** of PGWP holders reported T4 employment income (i.e., were employed)
- **Top sectors** where PGWP holders work: Retail Trade, Food Services and Accommodation, Healthcare
- **Field alignment gap**: A significant portion of PGWP holders work in sectors not aligned with their field of study — indicating that simply having a PGWP doesn't guarantee field-relevant employment

## NOC TEER System — What Qualifies for CEC

The National Occupational Classification (NOC) TEER system determines whether Canadian work experience counts for CEC/Express Entry:

- **TEER 0**: Management occupations (e.g., IT manager, engineering manager)
- **TEER 1**: Occupations requiring a university degree (e.g., software engineer, financial analyst, nurse)
- **TEER 2**: Occupations requiring college diploma + apprenticeship (e.g., computer network technician)
- **TEER 3**: Occupations requiring some secondary school or training (e.g., accounting clerk)
- **TEER 4**: Little or no formal education required (e.g., retail sales clerk)
- **TEER 5**: Low-skill labour

**CEC eligibility**: Work in NOC TEER 0, 1, 2, or 3 counts toward CEC requirements. Work in TEER 4 or 5 does not count for CEC, even if full-time and legal. This matters because many PGWP holders work in TEER 4/5 jobs in retail or food service — this employment, while legal and helpful financially, does not accumulate CEC-eligible experience.

**Key implication**: A PGWP holder working at a grocery store (TEER 5) for 2 years is NOT accumulating CEC-eligible experience. They must secure TEER 0-3 employment to build their Express Entry profile.
