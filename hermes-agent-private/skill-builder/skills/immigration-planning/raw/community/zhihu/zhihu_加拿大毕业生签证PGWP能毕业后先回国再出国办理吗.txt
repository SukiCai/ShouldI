最佳回答：
1. 你的DLI要符合申请PGWP的资格。 2.PGWP的时间长度： 2 years or more a PGWP may be issued for 3 years 如果是2年以上的全日制学习课程，则可获得长达3年的PGWP. 3.注意护照的有效期：If your passport expires before your PGWP should Make sure your passport is valid for the entire length of time you should be eligible for. If your passport expires earlier: we’ll only issue you a PGWP that’s valid until your passport expires 你要保证护照的有效期长度能完全覆盖PGWP的长度，否则就按照护照截止期来，不划算。 4.申请PGWP的时间节点：You have 180 days  after your school issues your final marks to apply for a post-graduation work permit (PGWP). 也就是在拿到毕业证明的180天之内申请即可。 5.境内还是境外申请：You can apply for a post-graduation work permit (PGWP) from inside Canada or overseas, as long as you meet the eligibility. 也就是说， 既可以境内申请，也可以境外申请。 6. 最后注意： If you plan on staying in Canada and your study permit will expire before you apply for a PGWP, you should change your status to a visitor . You have up to 90 days after your study permit expires to restore your status or you’ll have to leave the country. 如果你毕业后还想停留加拿大，你的学习许可在你申请PGWP之前将过期，你应该转变身份为访问者身份，这个可以在你的学习许可截止期后的90天内来处理。 所以，你可以持学生签入境。（准备好相关材料。） 祝你顺利！
