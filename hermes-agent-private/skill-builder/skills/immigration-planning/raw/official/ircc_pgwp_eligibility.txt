TITLE: Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP): Who Can Apply — Canada.ca

== General Eligibility ==

To be eligible for a post-graduation work permit (PGWP), you must meet all eligibility requirements.

You meet the general eligibility requirement if:
- You completed a program of study at a PGWP-eligible designated learning institution (DLI) that was at least 8 months long (or 900 hours for Quebec programs)
- You maintained full-time status as a student in Canada during each semester of your study program (you may study part-time during your final semester)
- You apply for your PGWP within 180 days of confirmation that you completed your program of study
- Your study permit was valid at some point during the 180 days after you completed your program of study

== Accelerated Programs ==

If you complete your study program in less time than the normal length of the program, you may be eligible for a PGWP valid for the full length of your program. For example, if you complete a 1-year study program in 8 months, you may get a PGWP valid for 1 year.

== Distance Learning ==

Lock-in date on or after September 1, 2024:
- You must complete at least 50% of your program in class within Canada
- Time spent studying outside of Canada will be deducted from the length of your PGWP

Lock-in date between September 1, 2022 and August 31, 2024:
- You must complete at least 50% of your program within Canada (online or in class)
- Time spent studying online from outside Canada before August 31, 2024 counts toward PGWP length if it constitutes less than 50% of your program

== Curriculum Licensing Agreements (Public-Private Partnerships) ==

Programs delivered by a private career college on behalf of a public PGWP-eligible institution are generally NOT PGWP-eligible, with limited exceptions:
- Exception 1 (same province): Programs started on or before May 15, 2024 may be eligible
- Exception 2 (between provinces): Programs started on or before January 31, 2023 may be eligible

== Who Is NOT Eligible for a PGWP ==

You are not eligible if you:
- Already received a PGWP
- Studied English or French as a second language
- Took general interest or self-improvement courses
- Completed a non-credit program of study (except for a flight school program)
- Received GAC funding/scholarship requiring return to home country after graduation
- Completed over 50% of your study program through distance learning (online courses, e-learning, or correspondence)
- Completed a study program at a non-Canadian institution located in Canada
- Completed a course or program at a DLI that is not PGWP-eligible

== Eligibility Requirements Based on Study Program ==

Bachelor's, master's, or doctoral degree:
- Language requirement: Must prove English or French language skills with a minimum level of Canadian Language Benchmarks (CLB) / Niveaux de compétence linguistique canadiens (NCLC) 7 in all four language skills (reading, writing, listening, speaking)
- Field of study requirement: Your program must be in an eligible field of study as determined by IRCC

College diploma / graduate certificate programs:
- Must be in an eligible field of study AND language level CLB/NCLC 7 (if applicable)

== PGWP Duration ==

PGWP length is tied to the length of your study program:
- Program 8 months to less than 2 years: PGWP valid for same length as program
- Program 2 years or more: PGWP valid for 3 years
- Master's or doctoral degree (any length): PGWP valid for 3 years (since February 2024 rule change)

== Transfers Between Schools ==

If you transferred between DLIs, the length of your studies must be at least 8 months total to be eligible.
If you transferred from a non-DLI to a DLI, only the time spent at the DLI counts. You must spend at least 8 months at the DLI.

== Canadian Study Programs with an Overseas Component ==

If your program has an overseas component, the PGWP will only be valid for the length of time you studied in Canada (not the overseas portion).
