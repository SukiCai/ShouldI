## Overview: Canada's Tri-Council Graduate Funding System

Canada's federal research funding is administered through three councils:
- **NSERC** (Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council): science, engineering, mathematics, computer science
- **SSHRC** (Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council): social sciences, humanities, arts
- **CIHR** (Canadian Institutes of Health Research): health sciences, biomedical research

All three now administer a unified program for doctoral awards called the **Canada Graduate Research Scholarship – Doctoral (CGRS-D)**.

---

## Canada Graduate Research Scholarship – Doctoral (CGRS-D)

### Formerly known as:
- CGS-D (Canada Graduate Scholarship – Doctoral)
- PGS-D (Postgraduate Scholarship – Doctoral)
- SSHRC Doctoral Fellowship
These have been merged into the unified CGRS-D program.

### Award value and duration:
- **Amount**: CAD $40,000 per year
- **Duration**: Up to 36 months (3 years)
- **Total maximum**: CAD $120,000

### Eligibility:
- Canadian citizens, permanent residents, and protected persons
- International students may receive up to 15% of available awards — must be enrolled at a Canadian institution
- Must have completed no more than 36 months of full-time doctoral study by December 31 of application year
- Cannot have previously received a doctoral-level scholarship from CIHR, NSERC, or SSHRC
- Maximum three lifetime applications per person

### Application:
- Submitted through Canadian institution (if institution has a quota) or directly to the agency
- Deadlines: Institutional deadlines vary; direct agency submissions due October 17 (8:00 PM ET)
- Results announced: April 30
- Applications through ResearchNet

### Selection criteria (equal weight):
- **Research Potential (50%)**: proposal quality, methodology, significance, originality, critical thinking, ethical conduct, communication
- **Experience & Achievements (50%)**: academic record, scholarships received, teaching/mentoring, project management, community engagement

### Agency assignment:
- NSERC administers CGRS-D for: natural sciences and engineering
- SSHRC administers CGRS-D for: social sciences, humanities, arts
- CIHR administers CGRS-D for: health research
- Applicants apply to the agency matching their research discipline

### Special provisions:
- Up to 10 additional awards reserved for self-identified Black student researchers
- Indigenous applicants may receive consideration beyond institutional quotas
- Award can be held at international institutions (limited to 20% of total awards; citizenship restrictions apply)

## Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships

### Overview:
Canada's most prestigious doctoral scholarship — named after Governor General Georges Vanier.

### Award value:
- **Amount**: CAD $50,000 per year
- **Duration**: Up to 3 years
- **Total maximum**: CAD $150,000

### Eligibility:
- Must have completed no more than 20 months of full-time doctoral study as of May 1 of the application year
- Must demonstrate: leadership ability AND high academic achievement AND research potential
- Available to both Canadian and international students at Canadian institutions
- Cannot have previously received a doctoral-level scholarship from CIHR, NSERC, or SSHRC

### Selection criteria (equal weight):
- Academic excellence
- Research potential
- Leadership ability

### Process:
- Institution nominates — students cannot apply directly
- Institution submits nominations on behalf of candidates
- Number of nominations per institution is limited

### Comparison to CGRS-D:
| Feature | CGRS-D | Vanier |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Annual amount | $40,000 | $50,000 |
| Who applies | Student directly | Institution nominates |
| Competition | More accessible | Highly selective |
| Eligibility window | Up to 36 months in | Up to 20 months in |

## CGS-M (Canada Graduate Scholarship – Master's)

- **Amount**: CAD $17,500 per year
- **Duration**: 12 months
- NSERC, SSHRC, and CIHR each administer their own Master's stream
- Administered through institutional quotas

## 2024 Budget Changes: Major Increase Announced

Canada's 2024 federal budget allocated CAD $825 million over five years to address two decades of graduate wage stagnation:

**New proposed targets:**
- Master's stipends: CAD $27,000/year (up from $17,500)
- PhD stipends: CAD $40,000/year (up from $24,000 average)
- Postdoctoral fellowships: CAD $70,000/year (up from ~$45,000)

**Critical limitation**: Only Tri-Council grant recipients will see the increase. The majority of graduate students funded through supervisor research grants are excluded.

As noted by Professor Bruce Arndtsen (McGill University): "Many departments needed to top these fellowships up from research grants simply to get to our normal stipend level. Most chemistry graduate students don't receive these top awards."

Source: Chemistry World, 2024

## Strategic Implications for Grad School Selection

### If you're applying to Canadian PhD programs:
1. Apply for CGRS-D in your first year if eligible — it significantly supplements supervisor funding
2. Ask during admission: "What percentage of your PhD students receive Tri-Council funding?" (Indicates program competitiveness and student caliber)
3. Vanier is institution-nominated — if you're competitive, ask if the department nominates candidates and whether you'd be considered
4. International students: verify whether your discipline's agency includes international students in their 15% allocation

### Field-specific advice:
- **STEM/CS/Engineering**: Apply to NSERC CGRS-D
- **Social sciences/Economics/Psychology**: Apply to SSHRC CGRS-D
- **Health/Medicine/Neuroscience**: Apply to CIHR CGRS-D
- **Interdisciplinary**: Choose agency based on primary discipline; submit to one only
