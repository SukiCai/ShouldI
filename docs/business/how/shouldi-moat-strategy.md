# ShouldI Strategy & Moat Bible (Where)

## 1) Strategic Objective

Build the Judgment Intelligence category and become the default Judgment Layer for high-impact human decisions.

---

## 2) Market Thesis

* knowledge is abundant
* execution is increasingly automated
* judgment becomes the highest-value human skill

Therefore:

Products that compound human judgment will become a foundational software layer.

---

## 3) GTM Wedge

### Initial wedge

Professionals making career decisions.

First user cohort: software engineers navigating offer, switch, and compensation decisions.

Why this cohort first:

* high frequency
* high emotional intensity
* measurable outcomes
* strong willingness to pay

### Expansion sequence

career -> money -> relationships -> health -> entrepreneurship -> life planning

Rule:

Do not expand domains until retention and quality thresholds are met in current wedge.

---

## 4) Flywheel System

### 4.1 Data Flywheel

More decisions -> better Decision DNA -> better recommendations -> better outcomes -> higher trust -> higher retention -> more decisions.

### 4.2 Community Flywheel

More users -> more decision cases -> richer community signals -> higher product value -> more users.

### 4.3 Calibration Flywheel

More outcome validations -> better confidence calibration -> better guidance quality -> higher trust -> more outcome validations.

---

## 5) Moat Definition

Primary moat:

Longitudinal judgment data and Decision DNA updates across real outcomes.

Not moat:

* prompt tricks
* single-model performance
* UI novelty

Why durable:

Generic assistants rarely capture full chain:

question -> constraints -> prediction -> action -> outcome -> reflection -> model update.

ShouldI does.
