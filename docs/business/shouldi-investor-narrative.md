# ShouldI Investor Narrative

## 1) Vision
We believe every person will eventually have a lifelong AI that understands how they judge, not just what they ask.

Today, AI remembers conversations.

Tomorrow, AI should understand how you make decisions, where you are biased, and how to help you continuously improve judgment quality.

ShouldI is building the first **Judgment Intelligence** platform: a lifelong system that compounds human judgment over time.

## 2) One-liner
ShouldI helps people make important decisions with confidence, and every decision makes the next one better.

Not another AI chatbot.  
Not another advice forum.  
A lifelong Judgment Intelligence system.

## 2.1) Category-defining framing
Google organized the world's information.  
OpenAI organized intelligence.  
ShouldI organizes human judgment.

AI made knowledge abundant.  
Judgment becomes the scarce resource.  
ShouldI builds lifelong Judgment Intelligence.

## 3) The Problem
People do not struggle because they lack information.

They struggle because they make decisions with incomplete reasoning.

Today's options are broken:

- **ChatGPT**
  - Answers questions
  - Does not understand how you make decisions
  - Forgets why previous decisions succeeded or failed

- **Reddit / Friends**
  - Opinions are emotional, biased, inconsistent
  - No structure
  - No accountability

- **Productivity Apps**
  - Capture thoughts
  - Do not improve thinking

The real problem is not access to advice.

It is the inability to build better decision-making over time.

## 4) Our Insight
The next foundational AI layer is not conversation.

It is judgment.

The next generation of AI will not simply answer questions.

It will continuously improve how humans judge.

ShouldI focuses on one narrow but universal cognitive task:

**Decision making as a path to judgment improvement.**

## 5) Product
ShouldI has three reinforcing loops.

### Loop 1 — Explore
Users discover real-world dilemmas.

Instead of endlessly consuming content, they consume decisions.

Users:
- vote
- predict outcomes
- compare perspectives
- discuss reasoning

The goal is not entertainment.

It is building decision intuition.

### Loop 2 — Decide
Instead of asking an AI, users enter a structured decision process.

ShouldI interviews the user, collects constraints, identifies missing information, and analyzes behavioral tendencies.

Then it generates a recommendation using:
- multiple domain experts
- behavioral science frameworks
- community signals
- personal history

Output includes:
- recommendation
- reasoning
- confidence
- tradeoffs
- uncertainty
- next action

This is not answer generation.

This is structured decision generation.

### Loop 3 — Learn
This is our moat.

Every important decision eventually produces an outcome.

Users return months later.

ShouldI compares:

Expected outcome  
-> Actual outcome  
-> What happened?  
-> Why?  
-> What pattern does this reveal?

Instead of storing conversations, ShouldI builds a **Decision Graph** that evolves into **Decision DNA**.

The system gradually learns:
- risk tolerance
- recurring blind spots
- emotional triggers
- planning accuracy
- common biases
- confidence calibration

Over years, ShouldI becomes increasingly personalized.

Narrative progression:

Decision Memory  
-> Decision Graph  
-> Decision DNA  
-> Lifelong Judgment Intelligence

## 6) Why We Are Different
Most AI companies optimize answers.

We optimize judgment quality.

ChatGPT remembers conversations.

ShouldI remembers and improves decision patterns.

That is a fundamentally different compounding data asset.

## 7) Behavioral Intelligence (instead of Psychology)
We do not attempt clinical psychological diagnosis.

Instead, ShouldI models evidence-based behavioral decision patterns.

Examples:
- loss aversion
- confirmation bias
- planning fallacy
- status quo bias
- overconfidence
- risk preference

The system uses these patterns to ask better questions, surface blind spots, and improve decision quality.

Our differentiation is not multiple AI agents.

Every decision updates the user's Decision DNA graph, making every future recommendation more personalized and more calibrated.

The product compounds.
